Our Methodology

How TruthLens Works

Our hybrid verification pipeline couples natural language processing with live external evidence cross-referencing.

01

Multi-Modal Ingestion

Accepts text, URLs, screenshots via optical OCR, documents, and live microphone speech.

02

Claim Decomposition

Identifies key factual assertions, statistical figures, quotes, and named entities while discarding rhetorical padding.

03

Evidence Cross-Reference

Scans peer-reviewed databases, government registries, and certified fact-checking organizations for corroborated records.

04

Dual-Engine Triangulation

Combines calibrated ML stylistic probability with external evidence stance to output a transparent confidence rating.

About TruthLens

Built for Independent Media Accountability

In an era where synthetic disinformation and viral chain messages spread faster than verified reporting, TruthLens provides citizens, researchers, and journalists with an accessible, multi-modal verification cockpit.

Unlike black-box classifiers that generate opaque binary labels, TruthLens anchors every verdict to transparent primary sources. We strictly refuse to guarantee 100% certainty, recognizing that rigorous fact-checking requires constant calibration, primary documentation, and independent human judgment.
